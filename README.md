# 🛒 Superstore Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌Project Description

This project analyzes retail sales data from a Superstore dataset to uncover key business insights related to sales performance, product categories, regional trends and customer purchasing behavior. The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor KPIs, identify growth opportunities and optimize business strategies using data-driven insights.

## ❗Business Problem
- Lack of visibility into sales performance across regions
- Difficulty in identifying high-performing product categories
- Limited understanding of monthly and yearly sales trends
- No centralized dashboard for business decision-making

## 🎯Key Objectives
- Analyze total sales performance across years (2014–2017)
- Identify top-performing product categories
- Compare regional sales performance
- Track monthly sales trends and seasonality
- Provide actionable insights for business growth

## 🛠Tech Stack

- Excel → Data Analysis & Cleaning
- Power BI → Interactive Dashboard & Visualization

## 📊Dataset

Download the dataset [Link](https://github.com/RohitJain173/Superstore/blob/main/Sample%20-%20Superstore.csv)

The dataset contains transactional sales data including:
- Order Date
- Sales Amount
- Product Category (Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology)
- Region (West, East, Central, South)
- Product Details
- Year & Month

## 🚀Key Features
- 💰 Total Sales KPI (2.30M overall sales)
- 🏆 Best Selling Category → Technology
- 📉 Lowest Sales Month → February
- 🌍 Top Region → West
- 📊 Year-wise Sales Comparison (2014–2017)
- 📈 Monthly Sales Trend Analysis
- 🧾 Category-wise Sales Distribution
- 🗺️ Region-wise Sales Breakdown
- 🎯 Interactive Filters (Category, Region)

## 📂Project Files
- 📊 Power BI Dashboard: [Download PBIX](https://github.com/RohitJain173/Superstore/blob/main/Superstore.pbix)

## 📊Key Insights

- 📈 Technology category generated the highest revenue
- 🌍 West region contributed the most to total sales
- 📊 Sales showed consistent growth from 2014 to 2017
- 📉 February recorded the lowest sales across years
- 📅 Sales fluctuate significantly on a monthly basis, indicating seasonality
- 🧾 Furniture and Office Supplies contribute stable but lower revenue compared to Technology

## 📌Conclusion

This project demonstrates how retail sales data can be transformed into actionable insights using Power BI. By analyzing category performance, regional trends and time-based sales patterns, the dashboard helps businesses identify growth opportunities and optimize decision-making. The insights can be used to improve inventory planning, marketing strategies and regional focus.
